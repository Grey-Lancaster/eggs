Quail egg shells are thin but surprisingly tough, and their small size makes them tricky to crack cleanly with a regular knife or by hand. These small, sharp-tipped scissors snip off just the top of the shell in one clean cut, so the egg stays intact underneath.

Great for deviled quail eggs, garnishes, snacking, or any recipe where you want a neat, uncracked white and yolk. Compact enough to keep in a kitchen drawer and easy to hold and control.
